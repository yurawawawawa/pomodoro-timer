# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JS.

## Users

Students preparing for exams or engaging in deep work sessions.

## Product Purpose

To provide a focused, distraction-free environment that helps users manage their time using the Pomodoro technique while tracking tasks.

## Positioning

A focused Pomodoro timer seamlessly integrated with a study list, keeping distractions minimal compared to overly complex productivity dashboards.

## Operating Context

Used during study sessions, likely on desktop or tablet browsers while reading, researching, or writing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must retain the current background music (`lagu.mp3`) and alarm sound (`Alarm.mp3`).
- Must maintain the vanilla JavaScript implementation.
- Features include a 50/10 timer mode, a sticky-note task list, and basic background audio controls.

## Brand Commitments

The visual world should lean into a "classic study aesthetic vibe," deliberately moving away from generic styles.
